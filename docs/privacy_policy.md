# Trencadis Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Overview

Trencadis is a real-time audiovisual synthesizer that transforms camera input into generative music and cubist mosaic art. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data and permissions.

## Data Collection

**Trencadis does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

### Camera Data
- Camera feed is processed locally on your device in real-time
- No images or video are saved, stored, or transmitted
- Pixel data is analyzed only for immediate audiovisual synthesis

### Audio Recording
- Microphone input is used only for real-time audio synthesis
- No audio recordings are saved, stored, or transmitted
- Audio processing happens entirely on your device

### Bluetooth MIDI
- Bluetooth connectivity is optional and only used for external MIDI controllers
- No Bluetooth data is collected or transmitted
- Connection is established only when explicitly enabled by the user

## Permissions Used

- **Camera:** Required for real-time video analysis and visual synthesis
- **Microphone:** Required for audio input processing and synthesis
- **Bluetooth:** Optional, for connecting external MIDI controllers

## Data Storage

The only data stored on your device are:
- User-created presets (audio/visual configurations)
- App settings and preferences

All stored data remains on your device and is not accessible to us or third parties.

## Third-Party Services

Trencadis does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

## Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there are no data security risks related to external data handling.

## Children's Privacy

Trencadis does not collect any personal information, making it compliant with children's privacy regulations.
